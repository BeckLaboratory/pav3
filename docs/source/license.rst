License and citation
====================

Citation
--------

PAV 3 does not yet have its own citation. A paper describing PAV 3 is in preparation and is
expected mid-2026. Until then, cite the paper for earlier PAV versions:

   Ebert et al., "Haplotype-Resolved Diverse Human Genomes and Integrated Analysis of Structural
   Variation," *Science*, February 25, 2021, eabf7117,
   https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abf7117 (PMID: 33632895).

Please discuss with the authors prior to publishing results generated with PAV 3; see
`contact information <https://www.jax.org/people/peter-audano>`_.
